Page lab — snapshot, quote and anecdote pages

Every page below is the exact HTML the PDF exporter emits, dropped into a box the real size of a page with the real content margin — so where things sit and how much room they take is what will print. The dashed line is the trim: anything outside it gets cut off.

mPDF is not a browser, though. Font metrics, table column widths and line breaking differ a little, so treat this as composition and proportion rather than typography to the millimetre. The page shape and the margins are exact; the type will be close.
