Layout Lab 2 — shape-driven

Templates transcribed from your sticky notes; a slot only ever receives the shape it asks for. Photos keep their own ratio except where two of the same shape sit side by side, which are matched to each other. Page is a true 8.5″ square.

Margins

Matched shapes draws a row or column of same-orientation photos at one identical size, centre-cropping any that miss the target ratio. A mixed row — a landscape beside a portrait — is left alone and simply makes a rectangle, so the lower band of a 2×2 can be shorter than the upper. Ratio widths crops nothing and lets every photo set its own width. Equal gaps gives every photo the same area, so the outer edge goes ragged.

Pages that crop are called out under the page, with how much of the photo is lost. Cyan template names are ones I derived to fill gaps your sketches don't cover. A dashed magenta edge marks a page pairing two lone photos from different events.
